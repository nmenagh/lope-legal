Lope Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

Lope is a personal envelope budgeting application available on iOS, Android, and web. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Lope.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Email address and password for account creation
  • Budget categories (envelopes), transaction amounts, payee names, and notes you manually enter

Information collected via Plaid (only if you connect a bank account):

  • Transaction history, account balances, and institution names
  • Plaid access tokens — stored server-side only, never on your device
  • We never see or store your bank username or password

Usage data collected automatically:

  • IP address, device type, operating system, and app usage statistics

Third-Party Service Providers

Plaid Technologies, Inc. — We use Plaid to enable bank account connections. When you connect a bank account, Plaid securely accesses your financial institution on your behalf. We receive transaction data and account balances from Plaid. We never see or store your bank login credentials. Plaid access tokens are stored server-side only and never exposed to the client app. Plaid's privacy policy is available at plaid.com/legal.

Supabase, Inc. — We use Supabase as our database and authentication provider. All user data is stored in Supabase's PostgreSQL infrastructure with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Lope budgeting service
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • To sync your budget data across your devices
  • To import bank transactions when you connect an account via Plaid
  • To send service-related notifications such as security updates
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing nathanmenagh@gmail.com. We will permanently delete your data within 30 days of your request.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Financial data and Plaid access tokens are stored exclusively server-side and are never exposed to the client application. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Lope is not directed at anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, contact us at nathanmenagh@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for bank account connections at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email nathanmenagh@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification. The Last updated date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.
