Lope Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

Lope is a personal envelope budgeting app for iOS, Android, and web.

Information We Collect

Directly from you: Email address, password, budget categories, transaction amounts, payee names, and notes.

Via Plaid (if you connect a bank): Transaction history, account balances, institution names. Plaid access tokens are stored server-side only. We never see your bank username or password.

Automatically: IP address, device type, OS, and app usage statistics.

Third-Party Service Providers

Plaid Technologies, Inc. We use Plaid to connect bank accounts. See plaid.com/legal.

Supabase, Inc. Database and authentication provider. AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.2+ in transit. See supabase.com/privacy.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain Lope
  • To authenticate your account
  • To sync budget data across devices
  • To import bank transactions via Plaid
  • To send service notifications

Data Retention

Data retained while your account is active. Email nathanmenagh@gmail.com to request deletion within 30 days.

Security

All data encrypted in transit via TLS 1.2+. Plaid tokens stored server-side only.

Children's Privacy

Lope is not directed at anyone under 13.

Your Rights

Access, correct, or delete your data anytime. Email nathanmenagh@gmail.com.
